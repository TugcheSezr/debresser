About us

Today's solution is tomorrow's success.

At De Bresser, we're more than just a removals and storage company; for over 100 years we've been ready to meet all your logistics needs. We're your reliable partner for everything to do with removals, storage, furniture projects, building management and asset management. Our goal is to optimise your business and living environment with our extensive range of services.

Whether you're a business owner operating nationally or a private individual with international plans, we offer tailored solutions that meet all your needs. We're also working hard on an ever more complete range of services for the future, so a single partner can help you with all your logistics questions.
