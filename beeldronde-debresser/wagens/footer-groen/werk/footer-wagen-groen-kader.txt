Composition for a very wide 21:9 website FOOTER BANNER. On a computer the picture is cropped to a thin horizontal band, only the middle half of its height, across the full page width, under a dark navy tint, with white text over the LEFT half. On a phone it is cropped to a 1.9:1 band around the centre, without text. So:
- The truck stands in the RIGHT HALF of the picture: the whole truck, nose to tail, lies between about 58 percent and 88 percent of the width from the left edge, its cab on the left end of the truck, toward the middle of the picture, and its rear well away from the right edge.
- The truck sits in the vertical middle of the frame and is about 30 percent of the frame height tall: its roof and its wheels are both well inside the middle half of the frame height, with a clear margin above the roof and below the tyres.
- The LEFT HALF is calm and fairly dark green: dense foliage, tree trunks and hedges in shade, grass in shadow. No bright sky, no white or light surfaces, no sunlit hot spots, no road markings and no second subject in the left half, so white text laid on top of it stays easy to read.
- The setting is clearly green and leafy: trees, foliage and grass fill most of the frame. Full summer leaf, fresh natural greens.
- The top quarter and the bottom quarter of the frame are simple foliage, grass or road (and sky only above the right half), because they are cut off on a computer.
