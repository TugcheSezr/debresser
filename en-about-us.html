De Bresser

About us

Our company has existed for more than 100 years. We started in 1923 as a transport company with a horse and cart, and over the years we've developed into a professional organisation with various specialisms. De Bresser B.V. is a member of Erkende Verhuizers and Top Movers Nederland.

The common thread running through our complete service is that we always aim for 100% satisfied customers. That's why with us, a deal is always a deal, and honesty comes first. We look for the best solution for your situation and keep improving ourselves.

De Bresser is led by Tomas and Ineke Brands. In 2011, Tomas took over the shares from the 3rd generation of the De Bresser family. Together, Ineke and Tomas make sure that a personal approach and the quality of our service remain top priorities. Tomas is Managing Director, and Ineke plays an important role in the back office.

History

De Bresser Verhuizingen BV, Top Movers, came about when sons Jan and Nico de Bresser took over their father Jan de Bresser's company in 1983. Jan de Bresser was a removal and transport company founded by Cis de Bresser in 1923, at the time located on the Joh. Lenartzstraat in Oisterwijk. Today, we're based at Schijfstraat 13 in Oisterwijk. This is parallel to the railway, opposite the station.

De Bresser is a recognised removal company. This means you're choosing a reliable removal company. Every recognised removal company meets all the requirements set by the Organisation for Recognised Removal Companies. This means you can be sure that all our staff are skilled professionals, so your belongings are moved responsibly. This covers safety, your household contents and the environment.
