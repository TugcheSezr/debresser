The most popular reason for moving house!

The most popular reasons for moving house are as follows; marriage or moving in together, moving to a bigger home, moving closer to work, and separations or a break-up. As you can see, these are generally all happy reasons to move house, although separations are of course sometimes unavoidable. To make a removal go as smoothly and stress-free as possible, it's wise to involve a recognised removal company in your move. It's of course very important that your household contents are handled safely and responsibly.

Marriage or moving in together

Tell your child in good time that you're moving house as a family. However, really take the time to explain everything calmly. This is even more important for young children, as they're less able to put things into perspective yet. Tell them around 6 weeks in advance. If you already have an older child, you can involve them too. Think about sorting through belongings: what's coming and what's not? This way you go into the removal process calmly.

Moving to a bigger home

Wow, you've reached the next stage, moving to a bigger home! Over the years you've gathered all sorts of belongings. Much of it will come with you to your new home, but some things will be left behind. First, sort out carefully what's coming and what's not. After that, it's wise to have an advisor visit and walk through your home together. The advisor takes stock of how many m³ need to move with you. Based on that, a no-obligation, tailored quote follows. Also ask about additional options; a removal company often offers several services. That way you can hand everything over and removal stress won't be an issue!

Closer to work

For years you've driven back and forth to work. That costs a lot of time and energy, and eventually you've come to the conclusion that you'd rather live closer to your job. This means letting go of your old, familiar surroundings and being open to a new town and a new neighbourhood. Since a whole removal is no small thing, it's wise to have a recognised removal company draw up a tailored quote, so you can see what the costs are and whether you might want to outsource this. You can arrange an appointment with an advisor, who will look at how many m³ need to move to your new home. An advisor often also has interesting tips on how to settle into your new neighbourhood quickly!

Separations or a break-up

Unfortunately, separations and break-ups are also part of life. Sometimes there's no other option, and you both go your own way. Your household contents still need to be divided, though. Who takes what? You'll need to sort this out together first. After that, it's smart to involve an advisor in the removal process. An advisor can assess how many m³ need to be moved. If one of you doesn't have a new place yet, it's also possible to (temporarily) store your household contents. Discuss the options thoroughly with your advisor; this can take away a lot of stress during this difficult period.
