    Assessing inventory

    At De Bresser we go further than simply taking inventory of assets; we assess them against various criteria to ensure a sustainable approach. Our process includes a careful evaluation of each asset on several levels:

    • Refurbished: We refurbish assets to extend their lifespan.

    • Reuse: Suitable assets are reused to reduce waste.

    • Repair: Defective assets are repaired to preserve value.

    • Repurpose: We look for new applications for assets that can no longer be used for their original purpose.

    • Recycling: Unusable assets are recycled to recover raw materials.

    • Recover: Residual materials are assessed for energy recovery or value recovery.

    With this thorough approach to asset assessment, we aim to help your business achieve its sustainability goals and contribute to a circular economy.
