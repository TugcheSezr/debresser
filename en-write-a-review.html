    About Erkende Verhuizers

    A removal is often a big step and a hectic period. With expert advice and versatile services, Erkende Verhuizers can make this event a success. They can help you before, during and after the removal with all sorts of services, such as disassembling and assembling furniture, or temporarily storing your belongings.

    All Erkende Verhuizers are affiliated with the Organisation for Erkende Verhuizers and meet several requirements in the areas of quality, environment and safety. Companies are assessed against these requirements by an independent body. In addition, Erkende Verhuizers are trained to handle belongings discreetly and with care.
