The sustainable work environment

Discover our newest service and enjoy day-to-day efficiency and complete peace of mind. Because of widespread working from home, for example, companies currently have surplus furniture, whether temporary or permanent. With De Bresser you can temporarily store this furniture at one location, and if furniture is permanently surplus, we also offer to buy it from you at fair prices.

Buying and selling assets

When furniture is no longer in use, De Bresser also offers the option to give it a new life by listing it on our online platform for circular furniture. Our wide range of used furniture offers a simple and transparent solution for both selling and buying existing furniture. We also provide full support with transport, removals and storage, as an integral part of our complete inventory assessment.
