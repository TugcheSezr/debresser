De Bresser Removals

A business or private removal, care relocations, international moving or transporting furniture: De Bresser is ready to help. With our professional approach and experienced team, we ensure a smooth process, moving your belongings safely and efficiently to their new destination.

A move is a big event for you and your family. Moving house often causes a lot of stress, but we can take all the worries off your hands. De Bresser is a member of the Organisation for Recognised Removal Companies, which means you can rely on important guarantees and a skilfully executed, responsible and worry-free move.

How we work

Our approach

  1. 1

    1. Intake / quote

    Discuss your move by phone, video call, or with a specialist advisor for an accurate quote. We prefer a personal assessment by one of our experienced advisors, so you won't face any surprises and we can offer you an exact quote.

  2. 2

    2. Packing

    Choose to pack yourself or let us arrange the packing for you. Let us know your preference in advance so we can deliver the necessary packing materials to your home.

  3. 3

    3. Final check

    A final on-site check to make sure everything runs smoothly on moving day. A specialist visits a few days before the move to go through the details and discuss any last-minute adjustments, preventing surprises on the day.

  4. 4

    4. Removals

    Confirmation of the moving times a day in advance, followed by professional movers who make sure everything runs smoothly. Our office staff call to confirm the moving times, after which our experienced movers make sure your move goes efficiently and worry-free.

  5. 5

    5. Aftercare

    Choose to unpack your belongings yourself or let us do it, with the option to have the packing materials collected and receive aftercare. Once most of the move is complete, you can choose to unpack your things yourself or leave it to our specialists. After the move, we're also on hand for any questions or comments you may have.
