Furniture assembly service

Our experienced assembly service is ready for you during delivery. From assembling wardrobes, beds and tables to electrically connecting lamps, our certified handymen get the job done without any trouble. With VCA certification and NEN certification, we guarantee professionalism and quality.

We also offer a complete packaging and assembly service for your furniture. Our skilled staff ensure safe packaging and repacking, so your goods arrive safely at the right destination. Our drivers have extensive experience assembling various pieces of furniture on site and make sure the waste from the protective packaging is neatly removed, so you can enjoy your order without any worries.
