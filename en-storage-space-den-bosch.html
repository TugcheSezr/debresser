    Storage spaces Den Bosch: we help with packing!

    At De Bresser's storage spaces in Den Bosch, we offer three different storage options. We are happy to give you the option of packing the container yourself with our DIY storage service. This way you can be sure the container is packed exactly the way you want. Another option is our household contents storage. Here you pack your household contents yourself, but we take care of packing the container. Finally, we can offer you a full service. This means we help you with everything, both packing your household contents and packing the container efficiently and smartly.
