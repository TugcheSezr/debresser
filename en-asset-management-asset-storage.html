Regional storage

Our regional storage facilities offer a safe and controlled environment for your assets, while our customer portal gives you full control and insight into the management of your stored goods. Together with you, we look at the shelf life and duration of storage, so we can always offer the most effective solution. We also use electric vehicles for environmentally friendly transport to and from our storage locations, reinforcing our commitment to sustainability.
