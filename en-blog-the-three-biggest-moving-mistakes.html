Moving mistakes

The three biggest moving mistakes

It's not for nothing that moving house is in the top 5 of stressful life events. There are a number of well-known moving mistakes: incorrect transport, the wrong moving boxes and starting too late. Fortunately, all of these mistakes can be avoided by hiring a recognised removal company! That way, a removal doesn't become a stressful event but a smooth and well-organised one! Isn't that what we all want?

The three biggest moving mistakes and how to avoid them!

Moving mistake 1: Incorrect transport

If you hand over your removal to professionals, the recognised removal company is responsible for the entire move. Because the removal company holds this quality mark, it has professional transport available, including moving lifts, and everything loaded into the removal vans is insured for transport. If you choose to transport your removal yourself, it's important to choose the right type of transport. Can your household contents be moved with a trailer or a van, or do you need a lorry with a tail lift? It's important to think this through carefully and measure your belongings, so you don't face any surprises on moving day. It's also important to properly map out the doorways in your old and new home. This way you can check whether all your belongings will fit through them. Otherwise, part of your household contents may need to be brought in and out through a window, possibly using a moving lift.

Moving mistake 2: The wrong moving boxes

Moving boxes are sold everywhere, but there's a big difference in box quality. A removal company can offer customers new boxes, but also used ones. This depends on the available budget. But removal companies reuse moving boxes several times for good reason. The boxes are very strong, don't tear and are biodegradable. On moving day, and while packing in the days before, it's also not very motivating to keep working with cheap, tearing boxes. This means preparation doesn't go very smoothly, so our advice is to get your boxes from a recognised removal company for a good preparation!

Moving mistake 3: Starting too late

Good planning is half the work! If you want to hire a recognised removal company for your move, they'll visit you for a conversation and an inventory of your household contents. This way, the removal company knows how many cubic metres need to go to the new home and can draw up a quote. A recognised removal company can also handle disassembly work, packing of household contents, handyman jobs and clearing items in and out. This way, everything is arranged down to the last detail! If you're carrying out the removal yourself, it's important to make a plan for what needs to be packed, when, and what will or won't be taken to the new home. Start packing your belongings early enough, so that everything stays neat and in good condition!
