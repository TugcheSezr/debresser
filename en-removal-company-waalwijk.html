    Frequently asked questions about moving house in Waalwijk

    What does a removal in Waalwijk with a removal company cost?

    A removal in Waalwijk with a professional removal company costs on average between €900 and €1,600, depending on the volume of items, the distance and extra services such as a packing service or furniture disassembly. In 2026, the hourly rate of a recognised removal company is between €35 and €60 per person. At De Bresser, you can request a no-obligation quote in advance, based on an inventory of your household contents, so you know exactly what to expect beforehand.

    Why choose a recognised removal company in Waalwijk?

    A recognised removal company is affiliated with the Erkende Verhuizers trade association and must meet strict requirements in terms of craftsmanship, insurance and complaint handling. This means your household contents are fully insured during transport, and in the event of a dispute you can turn to an independent disputes committee. De Bresser has been a recognised removal company for more than 100 years and, as a regional Brabant company, knows the roads and logistical ins and outs of Waalwijk and the surrounding area.

    How fast is Waalwijk growing, and what does that mean for the removals market?

    Waalwijk is a growing municipality that passed the mark of 51,600 residents in 2026, the highest number ever (CBS, 2026). Compared to 2025, 397 new residents were added, an increase of 0.77%. This growth results in an active removals market with a lot of regional moving activity between Waalwijk and neighbouring municipalities such as Tilburg, 's-Hertogenbosch and Heusden. A removal company with local experience in the region is therefore a clear advantage.

    You can arrange the most affordable removal in Waalwijk with a tailored service: you pack and unpack yourself and only outsource the heavy lifting and transport.
