Sustainable mission

At De Bresser Verhuizingen, we remain actively committed to a sustainable future. In 2025, too, we again took major steps to reduce our ecological footprint and further strengthen our social involvement.

Through targeted investments, conscious choices and the commitment of our employees, we have further expanded and deepened our sustainability goals.

    Sustainable removals 2025

    Working together towards a better world

    A selection of our initiatives from the past year to reduce our impact on the environment and promote social sustainability:

    • Waste processing

      In 2025 we achieved a waste reduction of 36% compared to 2024. Through further optimisation of return flows, better separation of materials and reuse of packaging, residual waste has been significantly reduced.

    • Electric vehicle fleet

      In 2025, our vehicle fleet was expanded with 3 additional electric vehicles. As a result, more than 15% of the total fleet is now electric. These vehicles are used for both urban and regional removals and make a direct contribution to further reducing our CO₂ emissions.

    • Packaging materials

      Thanks to the use of sustainable and reusable materials, we managed to save 35,000 moving boxes in 2025. Our cardboard boxes continue to be made from 100% recycled material and are fully recyclable. In addition, we continue to actively focus on minimising plastic packaging materials

    • Training and awareness

      In 2025 too, our employees actively focused on sustainable practices. Through refresher training and practical instructions, sustainability remains a fixed part of our daily work, both on location and on the road.

    • Sustainable partners

      We have further deepened our partnerships with sustainable partners. From our 6 hubs in the Netherlands and Belgium, we're increasingly handling deliveries on a decentralised basis, resulting in shorter transport distances, lower emissions and more efficient logistics for our customers.

    • CO2 compensation

      For emissions that cannot yet be fully avoided, we continue to invest in CO₂ offset programmes. In 2025 we again contributed to forest restoration projects and sustainable energy initiatives, both in the Netherlands and internationally.

    A second chance for textiles and household goods

    Our service, where customers can hand over old household goods and textiles for a second life, continues to grow. In 2025 we again contributed to extending the lifespan of materials and reducing waste. Reuse has thus become a fixed part of moving house. Together with our partner Tex.nl, we ensure that collected textiles are responsibly reused or recycled. This makes it easy for customers to make conscious choices during a removal, and together we contribute to a circular economy.

    Social involvement 2025

    Social involvement is an essential part of our vision on sustainable business. We believe that real sustainability goes beyond environmental impact alone and is also about people, communities and equal opportunities. By actively taking responsibility and committing to social initiatives, we aim to make a positive and lasting contribution to the society in which we operate.

    In total, we supported 17 charities and social initiatives, ranging from local projects to national organisations. In doing so, we actively contribute to an inclusive and engaged society.

    Looking ahead

    The results of 2025 form a solid basis for further sustainability efforts. In 2026 we will focus on, among other things:

    • Further expansion of the electric vehicle fleet
    • Even more waste reduction and circular removal solutions
    • Further optimisation of logistics processes
    • Intensifying partnerships with social and local initiatives

    Sustainability and social involvement are an ongoing process and a fixed part of how we work. The steps we took in 2025 underline our ambition to do business responsibly and with an eye on the future.
