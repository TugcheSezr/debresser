Care relocation

De Bresser is your partner for local, national and international removals.

+31 (0)13 52 82 372 info@debresser.nl

More than just moving

For seniors and people who need extra care, De Bresser offers a special care relocation service. With this service, we take the entire move off your hands, from start to finish.

Before the move, we hold a calm intake meeting to discuss your wishes and needs together. We help you select which household items to keep and make sure surplus items find a suitable new home, taking the endless lifting and organising off your family's hands.

Our removal service is very extensive and flexible: you decide which aspects you'd like. Besides packing, transporting and unpacking your belongings, we can, for example, also assemble furniture, remove flooring, arrange cupboards and pass on change-of-address notifications.

    Ontzorgd Verhuizen

    In partnership with Ontzorgd Verhuizen De Bresser offers various care services, including senior removals, care relocations, house clearance after a bereavement and resident removals. Ontzorgd Verhuizen specialises in senior removals, while De Bresser has more than 100 years of removal experience and our staff have completed special care training. This means we can respond precisely to the specific care needs of our clients.

    Services

    Did you know

    • Handyman services

      With a move, we also offer the option to help set up your entire home. We make sure a "handyman" is available to help you arrange your home and connect appliances. This means you can move into your home worry-free on the same day.

    • Sustainable

      As an extra service, De Bresser can also sustainably dispose of or recycle items and furniture you no longer need, so your old home is ready for handover.

    • Thebe Extra

      Taking better care of yourself and of each other is what matters most to the members of the Thebe Extra members' association. Members receive a discount on removals through De Bresser.

    • Partial removals

      Whether it's a single piece of furniture or part of your household contents, we arrange the transport and delivery for a partial removal.
