    A professional move in Dongen typically costs between €900 and €1,600, depending on the volume (m³), the distance and extra services such as a packing service or furniture disassembly. In 2026, the hourly rate of a recognised removal company is between €35 and €60 per person. At De Bresser, you'll receive a clear price indication in advance based on an inventory at your home, so you won't face unexpected costs. Always request multiple quotes: the difference between the highest and lowest quote averages €400.

    Why choose a recognised removal company for a move in Dongen?

    A recognised removal company is affiliated with the trade association Erkende Verhuizers and meets strict quality standards in craftsmanship, insurance and complaint handling. This means your belongings are insured during transport and that you can turn to an independent disputes committee if any issues arise. De Bresser has been a recognised removal company for more than 100 years and combines that reliability with local knowledge of Dongen and the Midden-Brabant region.

    How many people move to and from Dongen each year?

    Dongen is a growing municipality with more than 27,600 residents (CBS, January 2026). In the past year, around 1,209 new residents settled in Dongen, while 905 residents of Dongen moved to another municipality. Most moves are regional: Tilburg, Gilze en Rijen and Oosterhout form the top three origin and destination towns. This makes a removal company with local experience in the Dongen region especially valuable for a smooth move.
