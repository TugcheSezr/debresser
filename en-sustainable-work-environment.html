Building management

At De Bresser we make building management simple and effective. We not only take care of the maintenance and sustainability upgrades of your property, but also provide a trusted caretaker as your dedicated point of contact. With our approach, we aim to keep your office environment not only functional, but also sustainable and pleasant to work in. With our monthly quality scans, we make sure your building keeps performing optimally. We are here to make sure everything runs smoothly, with a focus on your satisfaction and reducing our impact on the environment.

Asset management

Range of circular application strategies:

  • - Refurbished
  • - Reuse
  • - Repair
  • - Repurpose
  • - Recycling
  • - Recover

1. Inventory

Good asset management starts with a thorough inventory. Our dedicated and experienced asset manager will go through your building from top to bottom and look at every piece of furniture, after which all your assets are mapped out precisely. In doing so, we aim not only to improve your operational efficiency, but also to minimise your CO2 footprint and actively contribute to a more efficient circular economy.

2. WMS and Furniture Passport

After that, we digitise your assets with our advanced Warehouse Management System (WMS), including detailed CO2 reporting. This allows us to register every piece of furniture and provide it with a Furniture Passport. This passport contains all the key information about a piece of furniture, so you can make well-informed choices about reuse, repair or recycling. This way we contribute to a circular economy by preserving value and minimising waste.

Range of WMS data components

  • - Asset tracking by quantity
  • - Asset tracking by description
  • - Asset tracking by location

Do you have furniture that has no immediate destination? See how De Bresser can support your business with your surplus furniture.

  1. 3. Asset storage

    Our regional storage facilities provide a safe environment for your assets, while our customer portal gives you full control and insight into the management of your stored goods. Together we look at the shelf life and duration of storage for the most effective solution. We also use electric vehicles for environmentally friendly transport to and from our storage locations, demonstrating our commitment to sustainability.

  2. 4. Buying and selling assets

    When furniture is no longer in use, De Bresser also offers the option of giving it a new life by listing it on our online platform for circular furniture. Our wide range of used furniture provides a simple solution for selling and buying existing furniture, with full support for transport, removals and storage.

  3. 5. Circular furniture

    In collaboration with local recycling centres and charity shops, we give leftover or defective furniture a new chance through repairs, renovations and upcycling. This way we actively contribute to reducing waste and promoting a circular economy. With our revitalisation programme, we strive for a more sustainable future in which value is preserved and waste is minimised.
