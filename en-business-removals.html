We help move your business

In the Netherlands, around 18,000 businesses relocate every year. This is according to figures from Ruimtelijk Planbureau.

Bringing your operational activities to a halt due to a removal can be quite a setback. We know that every second counts and that you want to get back to work quickly so you can serve your customers.

At De Bresser, we understand this all too well. That's why we work together with you on a tight schedule, so you're back up and running as soon as possible.

We at De Bresser will do everything we can to make your removal as pleasant as possible.

    Complete service during a business removal

    A removal involves more than just moving items from A to B.

    Preparatory and finishing tasks are essential, because there's still a lot to arrange after arrival at the new location. De Bresser's Handyman is a professional support for your facility organisation.

    With his help, we make sure your image stays at the right level during and after the removal.

    Of course, the Handyman has great practical insight and solid hands-on experience, and has a fully equipped service van at his disposal. We at De Bresser aim to make your business removal go as smoothly and problem-free as possible.

    Extras

    • Disinfection

      De Bresser offers the option to disinfect your workspaces, other rooms and contact points at very low cost. We do this using electrostatic cleaning with disinfectant. This cleaning can be provided daily, weekly or monthly.

    • Buy-up/storage

      If your furniture has become surplus, we offer the option to take it off your hands at a fair price. Do you have furniture that's temporarily surplus? Then you can choose to store it with us at one location. Your furniture is carefully registered via an online storage portal, where you can not only see what's stored but also place orders.

    • Deep cleaning of office chairs

      Now that office staffing is minimal, you can choose to have your office chairs deep cleaned.

    • Floor maintenance

      At De Bresser, we also offer floor maintenance. With our professional cleaning service, we can restore all types of flooring to perfect condition.

    Project removals

    With more than 100 business, school and care removals of over 100m³ a year, we're also your project removal partner.

    We also provide additional services for the business market, such as archive storage and management. You can also come to us for framework contracts for staff removals and Handyman services.

    With our removal service, we're not only active in the Netherlands, but also take care of business international removals.

    We have qualified project managers and handymen on staff who can perfectly guide any complex process, such as an internal move, from start to finish.
