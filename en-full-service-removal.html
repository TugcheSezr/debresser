Full service

This storage option is very similar to household contents storage. De Bresser comes to load the container for you in a professional manner. The difference with household contents storage is that De Bresser also packs your belongings for you.

De Bresser therefore takes the worry out of the entire removal and storage process for you.

    Everything arranged

    With De Bresser's full-service storage, an exciting removal becomes a relaxing experience; you don't have to worry about anything anymore.

    Unloading from storage

    Once you want your household contents back, just let us know, if a date for unloading hasn't already been agreed. Then we'll plan an appointment together to come and unload the container as soon as possible and unpack it completely again.
