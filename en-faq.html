Frequently asked questions

De Bresser is your partner for local, national and international removals.

+31 (0)13 52 82 372 info@debresser.nl
How far in advance should I book a moving date?

The earlier a date is known, the greater the chance that your preferred moving date is available. Our guideline is at least 8 weeks in advance. In the summer period this is a little longer, namely 10 weeks. Some days get booked up faster than others. Our advice is: once you know your moving date, let us know as soon as possible. A date can only be booked once!

Can I also receive a quote without an appointment with an advisor?

Certainly! However, we do prefer to arrange an appointment at your home. Why? We like to take the time to calmly work out which items from your household contents are moving to the new location. This also allows us to give better advice if there are large items that, for example, need a moving lift.

Moving house is also often a major undertaking. We can answer your questions and think along with you. Every removal is different, and we create a tailored approach for everyone so that everything runs as smoothly, safely and damage-free as possible.

Would you still like to receive a quote? You can do so by sending an email to info@debresser.nl we'll reply as soon as possible.

Do you also work on Saturdays?

In general, we don't work on Saturdays. If you have a special request to have your removal carried out on a Saturday, this is always possible in consultation. However, the cost of a Saturday removal is higher than a weekday removal. For more information, contact us via info@debresser.nl

When are the moving boxes and accessories delivered?

Whenever you like! We deliver weekly on Wednesdays in the Tilburg region and weekly on Thursdays in the Breda region. If you'd prefer to have the boxes and accessories at home weeks in advance so you can pack at your own pace, we'll make sure everything is delivered neatly on time. Call us to arrange an appointment. We deliver and collect the boxes once, at your home.

What is important on moving day?

On moving day, it's important that you gather all the house keys and keep them with you in your hand luggage. Take note of the meter readings and close the windows and doors. Once the removal team has arrived, the foreman will go through the plan with you and ask whether there are any particulars or matters that need extra attention.

Do we also need to provide the removers with food and drink?

Yes and no. De Bresser Verhuizingen greatly appreciates a cup of coffee or a soft drink for the removers on moving day, anything is more than welcome!

How do we know what time the removal team will arrive on the day of the removal?

At De Bresser Verhuizingen we always work with a day schedule. This means that the day before your removal, you'll hear from our office team what time our removal team will arrive. They always call you in good time to let you know how the day will look.

How is damage handled, should any damage occur?

We naturally handle your belongings with care, but it remains human work, which means mistakes can happen. Damage should be reported as soon as possible, within 2 weeks at the latest. The colleague responsible for damages will take on the case and get to work on it, and together we'll find a suitable solution.
