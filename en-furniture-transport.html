Furniture transport

Would you like to transport furniture from A to B? De Bresser is here for you. We're your partner for moving or transporting furniture, both nationally and internationally. With more than 100 years of experience in the removals industry, we ensure safe and efficient transport of your furniture to its final destination. Our lorries regularly travel through the Netherlands, Belgium and the rest of Europe, and thanks to our advanced software, you can track and manage your goods yourself.

Full service

From our logistics centre in Tilburg, we pack your furniture before it's loaded for transport. Our expertise covers various types of furniture, including restaurant, medical, school, hotel and luxury furniture. We offer full-service delivery, including bringing items into your home, unpacking, assembly and returning packaging materials.
