Remover

Tilburg | Oisterwijk | Breda | Venlo

+31 (0)13 52 82 372 info@debresser.nl

Remover

  • € 2.782,24 – € 3.692,32 (Collective Labour Agreement for Road Haulage)
  • 40 hours or by arrangement

A move is an important moment for customers. You make the difference.

We are looking for removers who take responsibility, are proud of their work and want to be part of a strong team for the long term.

What will you do?

  • Packing, loading and unloading complete household contents
  • Disassembling and assembling furniture
  • Working carefully and without damage
  • Professionally guiding customers on the day of the move
  • Working together in a fixed team you can rely on

You work at various locations in the Netherlands and sometimes abroad. No two days are the same.

    Who are we looking for?

    • You are physically fit and like to get stuck in
    • You enjoy working as part of a team
    • You are reliable and take responsibility
    • You want to develop yourself
    • You speak good Dutch

    Experience in the removals or transport industry is a bonus, but we consider motivation more important.

    We are looking for people who don't just want a job, but a place where they can grow.

    What do you get from us?

    • A good salary between €2,782.24 and €3,692.32
    • Full-time (40 hours) or fewer hours by arrangement
    • Opportunities to grow (for example foreman or driver)
    • Plenty of responsibility and freedom
    • A close-knit team with a down-to-earth, friendly working atmosphere
    • Company outings and drinks
    • Working for a stable family business with almost 100 years of experience

    We invest in people who want to stay with us.
