    Storage options

    There are various ways to store and/or move your household contents. Choose the option that suits you best.

    Full service storage/removal

    With De Bresser's Full Service Storage, a stressful move becomes a relaxed experience, and you no longer need to worry about a thing. Our experienced packers ensure your belongings are safely packed, while our experienced removal team guarantees a successful move. We load the container carefully, so that when unloading we know exactly where everything needs to go.

    When you want your household contents back, just let us know. If no date has been agreed for unloading yet, we are happy to schedule an appointment to unload the container as soon as possible.

    Household contents storage

    With this option, you pack the small items yourself that need packing. On the day of the move, De Bresser comes to load the container for you, so you do not need to worry about that. Our experienced removal team ensures your belongings are carefully packed and transported, so they come out of the container in the same condition as they went in.

    When you want your household contents back, just let us know. If no date has been agreed for unloading yet, we are happy to schedule an appointment to unload the container as soon as possible.

    DIY storage

    If you prefer to arrange everything yourself and only need storage space, De Bresser can also help. We offer two options:

    1. We place a container outside your home. You load it yourself, together with your family, friends or acquaintances. Once the container is loaded, call our office team and we will arrange collection and safe storage of the container.

    2. You come and load a container yourself at one of our locations, after which it is placed in storage. When you are ready to receive your belongings again, contact our office team to arrange an appointment for unloading. A De Bresser driver will bring the container to your door so you can empty it yourself. Once the container is empty, we will collect it again.
