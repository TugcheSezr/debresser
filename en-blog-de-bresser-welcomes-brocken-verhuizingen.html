De Bresser welcomes Brocken Verhuizingen

+31 (0)13 52 82 372 info@debresser.nl

Brocken Verhuizingen

We are proud to announce that Brocken Verhuizingen from Tilburg has become part of our organisation.
A great step in which we further expand our services, while Brocken's trusted service is fully retained.

Brocken Verhuizingen specialises in senior removals. A removal later in life is often more than just moving boxes. It is an emotional step, with many practical questions. Together with Brocken, we therefore continue to focus specifically on senior removals, with:

  • 01

    extra clear explanation and guidance;

  • 02

    a calm pace and clear agreements;

  • 03

    help with packing and unpacking, if desired;

  • 04

    careful handling of personal and cherished belongings.

With Brocken's knowledge and De Bresser's strength, we can guide seniors and their loved ones even better, from the first introduction to the very last box.

What does this mean for Brocken's customers?

For Brocken's customers, almost nothing changes in practice, and that is exactly the intention.

All of Brocken's staff simply stay on board and continue to work from the familiar location in Tilburg. So you will keep seeing the familiar faces. Barry and Betty have also found a great place within De Bresser. Their knowledge, experience and commitment to customers are therefore fully retained.

In short: Brocken's trusted service remains, but is now even stronger thanks to the support of De Bresser.

    Why Brocken fits so well with De Bresser

    At De Bresser, we place great value on personal attention, care and reliability. These are exactly the values Brocken has been known for over the years.

    Brocken specialises in removals for the elderly and seniors. This expertise fits perfectly within our vision of taking as much off our customers' hands as possible, at every stage of life.
    By combining our strengths, we can:

    • 01

      respond even better to the specific wishes of seniors;

    • 02

      offer more time and attention during the removal process;

    • 03

      offer extra support with preparation, packing and setting up the new home.

    More options, the same personal approach

    As part of De Bresser, Brocken gains access to extra resources and capacity, while retaining its own style. Think of:

    • a larger fleet and more removal capacity;

    • additional tools and materials to make moving even more comfortable;

    • reinforcement in planning, organisation and logistics.

    For customers, this means:
    the same trusted approach, but with even more options to make the removal go smoothly and worry-free.

    Moving forward together

    We are happy and proud to welcome Brocken Verhuizingen into De Bresser.
    With this step, we combine the trusted character of a local specialist with the strength and continuity of a larger organisation.

    Do you have questions about what this acquisition means, or what we can do for you?
    Please feel free to contact us, we are happy to think along with you about a removal that is truly worry-free.
