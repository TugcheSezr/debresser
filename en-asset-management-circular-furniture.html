The sustainable work environment

Discover our newest service and enjoy daily efficiency and complete peace of mind. Thanks to one dedicated point of contact who is always available. We are happy to work together towards a sustainable future, where circular furniture and electric vehicles are the norm. We go further than just delivery. At De Bresser we believe in taking everything off your hands, so you can focus on what really matters.

A second life

In collaboration with local recycling centres and charity shops, we make sure leftover or defective furniture becomes relevant again. Through repairs, renovations and upcycling, we give furniture a new chance, actively contributing to reducing waste and promoting a circular economy. With our revitalisation programme, we strive for a more sustainable future in which value is preserved and waste is minimised.
