International removals with De Bresser

Considering an international removal within Europe or overseas, for example to Switzerland, Germany or France? De Bresser arranges it from start to finish. With a modern fleet, our own experienced movers and fixed European routes, you're assured of reliable planning and careful handling of your belongings. From our base in Noord-Brabant, we handle complete removals, partial removals and groupage shipments for private individuals and businesses every day.

    International removals within Europe

    Moving internationally involves more than just transport. We think along with you about the right packing materials, safe loading, dismantling and assembling furniture, and the best route. Our furniture routes run through the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg several times a week, so we can plan quickly and efficiently. By combining trips, you benefit from competitive rates without compromising on quality. Moving to a big city or a small mountain village? We prepare every trip carefully, including access to town centres, parking permits and the right equipment on site.

    Is it a compact household or just a few pieces of furniture for a second home or office fit-out? With our groupage solutions, we transport smaller shipments on fixed routes. That's cost-effective and sustainable. For temporary bridging, storage is available in our secure warehouses. This keeps your planning flexible and gives you a clear overview of every step.

    Outside Europe and customs handling

    If you're moving overseas, we guide you through export documents, customs formalities and the necessary insurance. You always have one dedicated point of contact who oversees the planning and keeps you informed. That way you know exactly where your shipment is and when delivery is scheduled. Our partners at the destination are carefully selected for service and reliability, so your belongings arrive in the same condition as they left.
