      Your belongings to Curaçao

      Every container starts with a thorough survey

      Is your whole household going, or only the furniture and boxes you want to keep using in Curaçao? First walk through every room, the attic and the storeroom. The volume and size of your items and the packing required determine how much space your move takes.

      For a container to Curaçao, we discuss whether a container of your own or shared container space (groupage) suits your shipment. In a shared shipment, your belongings travel with other cargo. Ask us to confirm in advance which option is available and what consolidation and unloading mean for the schedule.

      Show us large cupboards, bikes and fragile items during the survey. You can also do this remotely with a video survey. That way our international team can assess the space and the work involved. For leaving the Netherlands itself, see our page on moving abroad (in Dutch).

      Things to arrange

      Receipts for recent purchases

      Bought furniture or appliances shortly before you leave? Mention them during the survey so they are listed separately. Keep the receipts with your documents, not in a moving box.

      Sharing with other cargo

      If you choose shared container space, your belongings travel to Curaçao together with other cargo. Ask what consolidation and unloading mean for your schedule. Have the chosen option confirmed before you make other arrangements.

      Bikes, cupboards and fragile items

      Make sure these pieces are clearly visible, also in a video survey, and say what needs extra protection. Such pieces get bubble wrap, corrugated cardboard and packing paper all round from our packing service (in Dutch).

      Packing and crates

      Large cupboards, bikes and fragile items, properly packed

      On the way to Curaçao, your belongings travel in a container of their own or with other cargo, so we pack every item securely.

When loading, we secure everything firmly in the container, and every box is numbered on the inventory list we draw up.

      Planning and costs

      From the port to your new front door

      You may already be in Curaçao while your furniture is still on its way. Tell us when your home is available and who can open the door. Keep things for the first few days separate from the shipment, so you have them as soon as you arrive.

      The ship's expected arrival is one step in the schedule. Import clearance and transport to your address follow after that. Have each of those stages confirmed separately. Photos of the street, any gate and the route to the front door help prepare the delivery.

      What does a move to Curaçao cost? That depends on the volume, the container choice, the packing and the access at both addresses, among other things. Ask which port and handling charges, and which delivery service, are included in your quote. Also have the quote state which import tax, if any, you pay yourself. And discuss how storage or waiting time is charged if the home or the import is not ready yet.

      Moving in practice

      Your household goods through customs

      Booking a container does not mean your belongings can enter tax-free. If you are moving from the Netherlands to Curaçao, you can apply for an exemption for used personal goods, subject to conditions. Curaçao Customs assesses your case.

      Photo: Evandria, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0. Background removed with AI. Download the edited image.

      Check the conditions and the documents for your own moving file before shipping.

      • Used or new?

        Curaçao Customs defines removal goods as used personal belongings that have been part of your household for at least six months. Tax is due on new goods. Tell us about new purchases so we list them separately on the inventory list, and keep the receipts. Even for items that look new, you may need proof that you have owned them for longer.

        Rules for removal goods (in Dutch)

      • Transferring your normal residence

        The exemption is linked to transferring your normal place of residence to Curaçao. Simply shipping furniture to a second home is therefore not the same as moving there yourself. Have your move checked against the conditions.

        See the exemption conditions (in Dutch)

      • Have the import date checked

        The customs brochure of April 2023 sets a period of six months after transferring your normal place of residence to declare your removal goods for import. Before shipping, have the date and deadline for your file confirmed, including for any later part shipment.

        Read the April 2023 brochure (in Dutch)

      Documents for departure and arrival

      Which papers does your move need?

      De Bresser draws up your inventory list and numbers every item and box. The list shows the contents of each box, and you receive your own copy. For the exemption application, Curaçao Customs also asks for the value of the goods and a signature. You supply those details, as well as the contents of any personal boxes you pack yourself.

      The application also requires, among other things, passport copies with the required admission stamp, proof of deregistration from your previous country of residence, and a Kranshi population register extract or a work permit. The application and import declaration must be complete. De Bresser handles all customs formalities and goes through the official Curaçao Customs document list (in Dutch) with you. Keep needed originals out of moving boxes. Dutch nationals usually need a Declaration of Automatic Admission, other nationalities a residence permit. Our Dutch-language emigration guide is for Dutch nationals.

      Curaçao is not part of the EU customs territory. Your household goods leaving the Netherlands therefore also need an export declaration and an inventory list, as Dutch Customs explains (in Dutch). We handle the formalities for the Dutch export and the import into Curaçao, and agree when your personal details and documents need to be complete.

      Packing for abroad

      Well packed for your international move

      For your move to Curaçao, we recommend letting us pack your belongings. You can easily do a few personal boxes yourself. Our movers pack crockery, furniture and other fragile items with care.

      Do it yourself

      A few personal boxes

      • You can pack a few boxes of clothes and personal items yourself. Agree with us in advance what you want to do yourself.

      • Tell us what is in your own boxes. De Bresser adds the contents to the inventory list, numbers everything and gives you a copy.

      • Let us pack crockery, glassware and items that need extra protection. See our packing tips (in Dutch) for boxes you pack yourself.

      • Keep your passport, medication, keys and things for the first few days separate. Do not put them in with the belongings we take.

      Prepare your packing

      Our advice

      • We protect your belongings all round with bubble wrap, corrugated cardboard and packing paper. We match the material to the item.

      • For fragile or unusually shaped items, we arrange custom crates where needed. When loading, we make sure your belongings are well protected in the container.

      • We advise on what can go to Curaçao and what needs extra attention at customs. We discuss any restrictions before the boxes close.

      Do I need to book a full container to Curaçao?

      That depends on your belongings and the available shipping options. In a shared shipment, your belongings travel together with other cargo. Have us confirm whether a container of your own or shared container space is possible, and what that means for loading, unloading and the schedule.

      What determines the cost of a move to Curaçao?

      Volume, container choice, packing and access at both addresses all count. Ask which port and handling charges, and which delivery service, are included. Also have it stated which import tax, if any, you pay yourself, and how storage or waiting time is charged.

      Which documents do I need for customs?

      The main ones are a complete, signed inventory list with values, the required passport copies and proof of your departure and residence in Curaçao. De Bresser draws up the inventory list, handles the customs formalities and discusses which personal documents you supply, using the official document list. Keep needed originals out of moving boxes.

      How are my belongings insured in transit?

      As an Erkende Verhuizer, we insure your belongings as standard up to €100,000. Agree how you declare special or valuable items.

      Can I also bring a car or pets?

      Mention a car, pets, alcohol or other special goods as soon as you request a quote. Separate conditions may apply. Have the requirements assessed first, before you add these items to the shipment.

      What if my home in Curaçao isn't ready yet?

      Discuss where your belongings can wait and how storage and later transport are charged. Some of them may stay in storage in the Netherlands for the time being. For a later shipment, have the import deadline and documents checked again.
