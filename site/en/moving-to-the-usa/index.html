      Your belongings to the United States

      What are you taking to America?

      The dining table, the bed, the bikes: what do you want to use again in your new home? Show us what is in the loft and the garage too. A complete survey lets us assess the volume and the packing your belongings need.

      For shipping furniture to America, we check which suits your belongings: a dedicated container or shared container space (groupage). Groupage means your goods travel with other cargo. Ask which option is available on your route and how consolidation and unloading fit into the schedule.

      Give us your US city, state and ZIP code, even if the house number is still to follow. A quote to the port of arrival does not automatically include transport to your front door. Our page on moving overseas (in Dutch) explains more about the preparation.

      Things to arrange

      What do you want to keep?

      Go through your home room by room and note what you want to use again in the US. Do not forget the loft, garage and shed. Unsure about a piece? Show it to us during the survey anyway.

      Travelling with other cargo

      Ask whether shared container space is offered on your route to the United States. Your belongings then share the container with other cargo. Discuss what consolidating and unloading mean for your departure date and delivery.

      Your US address

      Send us the city, state and ZIP code, even if the house number comes later. Ask whether door-to-door transport is in the quote. A price that ends at the port of arrival does not cover it by default.

      How much are you taking abroad?

      Count your belongings room by room. Have more rooms or items of your own? Add them.       Arrival and costs

      From arrival port to your US address

      A ship that has docked is not yet at your front door. Import clearance has to happen first; only then is your shipment driven to your home. Tell us when you get the keys and who will receive your belongings if you cannot be there yet.

      Does your apartment building have rules for the lift, the loading area or moving times? Ask the building manager in advance. Photos and measurements of the entrance help us judge whether large furniture will fit and whether extra handling is needed.

      The cost of a move to the USA depends on the volume, the packing, the transport method and the route to your address. Make sure the quote states which port charges, handling and delivery are included. Also discuss storage and any extra transport if your final address is confirmed later.

      Plan together

      From ship to front door

      Once the ship is in, customs clearance is handled before the drive to your home. Give us the date you receive the keys and the name of whoever will take delivery if you have not arrived yet.

      Your building's rules

      Living in an apartment? Check with the building manager about using the lift, the loading area and moving hours. Send photos and measurements of the entrance and stairwell so we can assess large furniture beforehand.

      What does the price include?

      Ask for a quote that lists port charges, handling and delivery separately. Will your final address follow later? Then discuss storage and any extra transport as well, so you know what may be added.

      Moving in practice

      Getting your shipment ready for CBP

      Moving to the US? American customs treats belongings shipped separately as a shipment of their own. Your own arrival, residence details and transport documents must match. Have the file checked before your goods leave.

      Photo: AskALotl, Wikimedia Commons, CC0. Background removed with AI for this design. Download the edited image.

      Check the conditions and the documents for your own moving file before shipping.

      • A file for unaccompanied goods

        CBP uses Form 3299 for a declaration claiming free entry of unaccompanied personal and household effects. Which parts you complete depends partly on your residency status. De Bresser arranges the customs formalities and goes through the details and attachments needed with you.

        View CBP Form 3299

      • Declare new purchases separately

        Do not tuck new furniture, equipment or items meant for other people in among your used belongings. Mention them during the survey and keep the receipts. Claiming an exemption does not mean every item will be admitted free of duty.

        Read the information on the import form

      • Prepare the Dutch export too

        For a move from the Netherlands to a country outside the EU, Dutch Customs asks for an export declaration as well as an inventory list. De Bresser takes care of these formalities and tells you which details to supply before loading.

        Read about Dutch export rules (in Dutch)

      Your moving file

      Your inventory list, drawn up by De Bresser

      Whenever goods cross a border, the inventory list is drawn up by De Bresser. We number all your belongings and note the number and contents of every box. A copy is yours to keep. Packing some personal boxes yourself? Let us know what is in them so they appear correctly on the list too.

      For the USA, the file covers both your own arrival and the arrival of your belongings. Keep your travel documents and details of your residence status to hand. De Bresser deals with the customs formalities and guides the preparation of CBP Form 3299. You supply the required details, supporting documents and signatures. In general you need a visa; which one depends on your nationality and the type of stay. Our guide to emigrating to the United States (in Dutch) covers the personal steps.

      Are some pieces of furniture staying in the Netherlands for now? Tell us before the list and the transport are finalised. See also how to prepare for moving abroad (in Dutch).

      Packing for abroad

      Well packed for your international move

      As movers to the USA, we advise you to let us pack your household goods. A few personal boxes are fine to pack yourself. Our movers pack crockery, furniture and anything fragile with care.

      Do it yourself

      A few personal boxes

      • You can pack a few boxes of clothes and personal items yourself. Agree with us in advance what you want to do yourself.

      • Tell us the contents of the boxes you pack yourself. De Bresser makes the official inventory list, numbers all items and records the number and contents of each box. You get a copy.

      • Let us pack crockery, glassware and items that need extra protection. See our packing tips (in Dutch) for boxes you pack yourself.

      • Keep your passport, medication, keys and things for the first few days separate. Do not put them in with the belongings we take.

      Prepare your packing

      Our advice

      We pack your belongings

      • We protect your belongings all round with bubble wrap, corrugated cardboard and packing paper. We match the material to the item.

      • Edges and corners of furniture get extra protection. We fill empty spaces with packing paper, so items have less room to move inside the packaging.

      • For fragile or unusually shaped items, we arrange custom crates where needed. When loading, we make sure your belongings are well protected in the container.

      • Our advice covers what you can take to America and which items need a closer look at customs. Any restrictions are discussed before the boxes are sealed.

      Does ‘moving to America’ mean the United States?

      Yes. This page is about moving from the Netherlands to the USA. You will find separate information for Canada and Latin America. Always include the country, city and postal code in your request.

      Can I book part of a container to the US?

      Ask whether shared container space is available for your destination. Your volume and the schedule help decide whether it is a good fit. Also ask us to confirm in writing what combining cargo means for departure and delivery.

      How much does a move to America cost?

      The amount of belongings, the packing and transport to your address together set the price. Ask for a quote that clearly describes port charges, import clearance, delivery and any storage. Import duties and extra work are not automatically included.

      Do I need to make the inventory list myself?

      No. The official inventory list is our job: De Bresser numbers everything you ship and notes what each box contains. You keep a copy. Just let us know what you put in your own personal boxes.

      Are my belongings automatically duty-free?

      No. US customs assesses the import based on your situation and the goods. The form for unaccompanied goods is a claim, not a guarantee. Have new purchases and special items assessed separately before shipping.

      What do we agree on insurance and storage?

      As an Erkende Verhuizer, we insure your belongings as standard up to €100,000. If your home is not available yet, we discuss where your things can be stored in the meantime and how that is charged.
